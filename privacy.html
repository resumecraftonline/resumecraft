Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains how ResumeCraft handles information when you use our website.

ResumeCraft may process information that you voluntarily enter while creating or editing your resume.

This information may include your name, contact details, education, skills, work experience and other resume-related information.

Information entered into ResumeCraft may be used to provide and improve the resume-building functionality of the website.

We do not use your resume information for purposes unrelated to providing the website functionality unless otherwise explained on this website.

Resume Information

Users should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive personal information into their resumes.

You are responsible for reviewing the information you include in your resume before sharing or downloading it.

Third-Party Services

ResumeCraft may use third-party services for website functionality, analytics, hosting or advertising.

Third-party services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to help protect information handled through the website.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee complete security of information.

Children's Privacy

ResumeCraft is intended to provide general resume-building functionality. Users should avoid submitting unnecessary personal or sensitive information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page.

Contact Us
