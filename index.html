RESUME GUIDE

ResumeCraft helps students, freshers and working professionals create a professional resume with an easy step-by-step process.

What Is a Resume?

A resume is a short document that presents your education, skills, experience and achievements to employers. A clear and well-organized resume helps employers quickly understand your qualifications.

What Should a Resume Include?

A professional resume can include the following sections:


  • Full Name and Contact Information
  • Professional Summary or Career Objective
  • Education
  • Work Experience
  • Skills
  • Projects
  • Achievements and Certifications

Resume Tips for Students and Freshers

If you are a student or fresher, focus on your education, skills, projects, internships, certifications and achievements. Keep your information clear and relevant to the opportunity you are applying for.

Resume Tips for Working Professionals

Working professionals should highlight relevant work experience, important responsibilities, measurable achievements, technical skills and certifications.
